Suggested plans

A proposal to group the features below. Change it freely; the plan column in each table is only a suggestion.

Pro

Everything a small business needs to take money and connect tools.

  • Stripe + PayPal, product / total fields, coupons
  • Email marketing pack, Zapier / Make, Slack, Google Sheets, SMS
  • Signature, repeater, calculations, likert, chained selects, image choices
  • Multiple + conditional notifications and confirmations
  • Save & resume, conversational forms, landing pages
  • Advanced entry management, Excel and scheduled exports, PDF
  • Form styler with presets, email templates, template library
  • AI form builder

Business

For agencies and stores that run forms as a business.

  • Subscriptions, other gateways, inventory
  • CRM pack, Airtable / Notion / Trello, WooCommerce, cloud storage, WPML
  • Front-end posting, user registration, directories and views
  • Approval workflows, abandonment recovery, moderation queue
  • Reports, user journey, quiz and polls
  • Role manager, REST API / MCP

Agency

Unlimited sites and client tools.

  • White label
  • Unlimited sites and multisite
  • Priority support
  • Everything in Business

How this was produced
  • Paid-plan evidence: the feature and add-on lists on the WPForms, Formidable Forms, Ninja Forms and Gravity Forms sites, Fluent Forms' feature list, and the paid-version teasers inside the installed WPForms Lite, SureForms and Forminator code (for example WPForms Lite's locked field list, SureForms' Pro-only signature / repeater / date-time and IP, country and keyword restrictions, and Forminator's abandonment and reports code).
  • The SureForms and Forminator pricing pages could not be read, so their chips come from the plugin code only. Fluent Forms does not mark Pro-only features on its feature page, so its chip means “listed in its feature set”.
  • “Sold as paid by” counts vendors, not popularity. Priority: High = 5 or more, Medium = 3–4, Low = 1–2. A low count can still be a good differentiator (for example Directories and Views only exist at Formidable).
  • “FormGlut today” was checked in the FormGlut Pro plugin: a renderer exists for signature, rating, rich text and uploads; other fields are only listed in its registry.
  • Plan and effort (S / M / L) are proposals for this codebase, not competitor data.
