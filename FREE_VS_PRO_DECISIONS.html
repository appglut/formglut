How each decision was made
  • Free when 3 or more of the other plugins give it away for free, or when it is a basic building block (per-form confirmation, notes, CSV export). Users compare against those free plugins.
  • Pro when competitors sell it in paid plans and at most one or two plugins give it away: payments beyond basic Stripe, most integrations, advanced fields, workflow, reports, design tools and AI.
  • Basic free, advanced Pro: Stripe one-time payments are free (Forminator, SureForms and WPForms Lite offer them free); subscriptions, coupons, quantity / shipping and other gateways are Pro. Webhooks, Mailchimp, HubSpot and Slack are free; the rest of the integrations are Pro. CSV export is free; Excel and scheduled exports are Pro.
  • “Free in other plugins” comes from the free builds installed next to FormGlut (Forminator, Fluent Forms, SureForms, MetForm, Form Maker, NEX-Forms, Ninja Forms, WPForms Lite, Formidable Lite, Gutena, Gutenverse). Treat it as a strong hint; confirm a feature in a plugin's own screen before relying on it.
